# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Restaurant guests discovering Servvia, exploring the menu and story, contacting the venue, and completing reservations or menu pre-orders on mobile and desktop.

## Product Purpose
Present Servvia's Middleterrean dining identity through rich imagery and make menu discovery and booking feel calm, trustworthy, and effortless.

## Brand Personality
Calm, cultivated, welcoming.

## Anti-references
Avoid decorative restaurant templates, cramped editorial layouts, excessive glass effects, bright luxury cliches, novelty interactions, and changes to established customer workflows.

## Design Principles
- Let food and place carry the emotional weight.
- Make every action readable and comfortably touchable.
- Use restraint and consistency to communicate quality.
- Preserve familiar journeys while refining visual detail.
- Reserve gold for actions, focus, and selected state.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard requirements, preserve visible focus, support reduced motion, maintain 44px touch targets, and keep body copy at 16px or larger.
