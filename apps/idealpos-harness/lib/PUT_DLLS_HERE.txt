Copy these files from the Idealpos installation into this folder before
building — see README.md "Build".

  IdealPos.Webit.Core.dll   <- IdealposService/IdealPos.Webit.Core.dll
  IdealPos.Data.dll         <- Idealpos/IdealPos.Data.dll
  IdealPos.Common.dll       <- Idealpos/IdealPos.Common.dll

These are the exact three assemblies IdealPos.Webit.Core.dll itself depends
on — no other Idealpos DLL is required for this harness.

They are the client's own already-licensed/already-installed software,
reused as-is rather than pulling new NuGet dependencies onto a restaurant
PC. They are deliberately not bundled with this project.

IMPORTANT — take IdealPos.Webit.Core.dll from IdealposService\, not from
Idealpos\. On DESKTOP-SOKKOQ7 as at 2026-09-03 the two differ:

  IdealposService\IdealPos.Webit.Core.dll  082cec04…  <- correct
  Idealpos\IdealPos.Webit.Core.dll         0ca0d20d…  <- different build

The same applies to IdealPos.Common.dll. Taking the wrong copy will build,
but will not match the assemblies the live POS actually loads.
